Apple Music restore
  1. Tap Add Restore Shortcut once, then Get Shortcut. It lands in Shortcuts on your phone.
  2. Tap Apple Music Backup on the playlist you want back. The file holds its songs, in order.
  3. Share that file to the HardlyListening Restore shortcut — or open the shortcut and pick the file.
  4. The shortcut creates the playlist in Apple Music with its own name and order, and tells you what it could not find.

One shortcut restores every playlist. The file is the backup; the shortcut only rebuilds it.
